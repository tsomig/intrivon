Sectors

Applications across sectors.

Industry economics, operating constraints and reporting requirements shape how each application is configured.

01

Logistics & supply chain

Evaluate network expansion, capacity, pricing and service levels. Model demand, operating costs and working capital to compare investment choices, improve margins and track their contribution to enterprise value.

02

Data centers, energy & digital infrastructure

Value data center investments across operating capacity, planned development and leasing. Assess power access, energy costs, capital expenditure and financing through scenarios, investment monitoring and reporting for developers, investors and lenders.

03

Industrials & multi-entity groups

Compare business units, operating performance and capital needs. Evaluate expansion, acquisitions and potential synergies through consistent financial models, helping leadership prioritize improvements and allocate resources across the group.

04

Public organizations & enterprises

Evaluate investment priorities, budgets and delivery risks. Compare project alternatives, track milestones and measure economic and service outcomes, giving decision-makers traceable evidence for resource allocation and accountable oversight.

05

Investment funds & financial institutions

Monitor portfolio companies and private investments. Connect valuation, cash flow, risk and financing scenarios to support investment reviews, credit decisions and consistent reporting for funds, lenders and institutional investors.

06

Real estate & development

Evaluate property and development investments across leasing, construction costs, funding and delivery milestones. Compare phased development options and test how market conditions and execution risks affect cash flow and value.

Core values

How we earn trust.

Value creation

We agree how success will be measured and assess the contribution to business performance. Priorities reflect the client’s objectives, with progress reviewed against outcomes that matter to the business.

AI driven

We use AI for relevant analytical tasks, with accountable human oversight. Its role and limits are defined for each use case, including when expert review is required.

Custom tailored solutions

Applications reflect the client’s workflows, permissions and operational requirements. We adapt the interface, analytical models and integrations to how the organization works and who uses the results.

Efficiency (time and costs)

We streamline recurring analysis and reporting, measuring time and cost savings against a baseline. Improvements target avoidable manual work and delays while preserving the checks needed for reliable outputs.

Accuracy

Sources, assumptions and calculations are documented so outputs can be checked. Validation includes reconciliations, consistency checks and clear treatment of missing data or uncertainty before results are relied upon.

Confidentiality

Access and data use follow agreed permissions and confidentiality requirements. We define who may view, process and share information, with responsibilities and controls appropriate to its sensitivity.
