Services

Valuation & value drivers

Model businesses, assets and projects. Identify the assumptions that drive value and assess how operating performance, financing and strategic choices affect investment outcomes and enterprise value.

Risk & scenario analysis

Stress-test financial and operating assumptions. Compare investment alternatives under different demand and capital structure scenarios to understand downside exposure.

Portfolio monitoring & capital allocation

Track investments and business units against expectations. Compare performance, risk and investment needs to support portfolio reviews and decisions on where to deploy capital.

Management & owner reporting

Growth & transaction intelligence

Forecasting & optimization

AI strategy & implementation

Sectors

Decision intelligence applications across sectors

Industry economics, operating constraints and reporting requirements shape how each application is configured.

LOGISTICS & SUPPLY CHAIN

Optimize networks and investment outcomes

Evaluate network expansion, capacity, pricing and service levels. Model demand, operating costs and working capital to compare investment choices, improve margins and assess how each investment contributes to enterprise value.

DATA CENTERS & ENERGY

Evaluate capacity, investment value and risk

For data centers, assess power availability, capacity expansion and lease assumptions. For energy businesses, model how changes in commodity prices, operating margins and financing costs affect project value, cash flow and capital allocation.

INDUSTRIALS & GROUPS

Compare performance and allocate capital

Compare operating performance and capital needs across business units. Evaluate expansion, acquisitions and potential synergies through consistent financial models, helping leadership prioritize the allocation of resources across the group.

PUBLIC ORGANIZATIONS

Prioritize projects and track public value

Evaluate investment priorities, budgets and delivery risks. Compare project alternatives, track milestones and measure economic and service outcomes, giving decision-makers traceable evidence for resource allocation, accountability and oversight.

FUNDS & FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS

Monitor portfolio valuation and risk

Assess how changes in cash flow and financing affect the value and risk of private investments. Use the findings to review portfolios, inform credit decisions and report consistently to stakeholders.

REAL ESTATE & DEVELOPMENT

Assess development cash flow and value

Evaluate property investments by modeling lease income, construction costs, funding needs and delivery schedules. Compare phased development options and test how market conditions and execution risks affect cash flow and value.

Core values

How we earn trust

We tailor each solution to your business, use AI with expert oversight and measure performance and efficiency. We check the accuracy of our analysis and protect your information.

Performance

We agree with you on what success looks like and how to measure it. We track progress against those measures to assess how the solution contributes to your business objectives.

AI-driven analysis

We use AI where it adds value to the analysis. For each task, we define what AI can do, where its limits lie and when an expert must review the results.

Tailored solutions

We design each application around your workflows and access requirements. We adapt the interface, models and integrations to the people who use the results and the decisions they need to make.

Efficiency

We reduce manual work and delays in recurring analysis and reporting. We measure time and cost savings against your starting point and keep the checks that make the results reliable.

Accuracy

We document sources, assumptions and calculations so you can verify the results. Before you use them, we reconcile figures, check for inconsistencies and explain where data is missing and how uncertainty affects the results.

Confidentiality

We agree with you on who may view, process and share your information. We set access controls and assign responsibilities according to the sensitivity of the data.
