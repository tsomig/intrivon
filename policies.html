Privacy Policy

When you contact INTRIVON through this website, the form requests your name, email address and message. You may also provide your title and company. This information is used to review and respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential engagement.

The contact form is submitted through FormSubmit and delivered to INTRIVON by email. FormSubmit processes submissions under its own privacy practices. Please do not submit confidential client information, passwords or sensitive personal information through this form.

For questions about information you have provided, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact gts@intrivon.com.

If you identify a potential security issue affecting this website, please report it privately to gts@intrivon.com. Include the affected URL, a description and safe steps to reproduce the issue.

Accessibility Statement

We aim to make the INTRIVON website usable across devices and input methods. The website includes keyboard-operable controls, visible focus indicators, image descriptions and support for reduced-motion preferences.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact gts@intrivon.com with the page, the difficulty and your preferred way to receive the information. This statement does not represent a certification of compliance.
